Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-08

Stride is a training and nutrition log with an AI coach, made by heeeelyeeee, an independent developer. This explains what happens to your data.

Where your data lives

Everything you log in Stride (chat messages, meals, workouts, sleep, heart rate, body measurements, goals, and data read from connected apps) is stored on your phone, in a local database. Sign-in tokens for connected services are kept in your phone's secure storage.

Your Stride account holds only your email address, a hash of your password, your subscription status, and a count of today's coach requests (deleted after a day). Each sign-in session also records your IP address and a technical client label, and the account keeps the time of your last sign-in; all of it is deleted with your account. Stride's server never stores your logs, health data, chats or photos.

What leaves your phone

Google Gemini API (the AI coach). When you send the coach a message, photo, file (a PDF or text file), voice memo or form-check video, that content is sent to Google's Gemini API together with a summary of your logged data: profile and targets, recent workouts, meals, sleep, heart rate and HRV, weight, symptoms you've logged, goals, and facts you've told the coach. It passes through Stride's server, which adds Stride's API key and does not keep or log it. Google processes it under the Gemini API terms on Stride's paid account, so Google does not use it to improve its products. Form-check videos are uploaded to Google temporarily and deleted automatically after about 48 hours. Photos and files you attach to a message are sent with that message only; a copy stays on your phone until you delete the message. If you've set a backup folder, a copy is saved there too, and Stride doesn't remove it from that folder; delete it there yourself.

Run routes and second-by-second readings stay on your phone. A run's GPS route and its pace and heart rate over time are used to draw charts and are never sent to the AI coach.

Location, only while you record a run. If you record a run in Stride, it uses your phone's location from Start to Finish to measure distance, pace and route. The route is stored on your phone with the run. Stride does not use your location at any other time and never in the background when you are not recording.

Open Food Facts. Scanning a barcode sends just the barcode number to look up the product.

Stadia Maps. To draw the map behind a route, Stride downloads map images for the area the route covers. Stadia Maps sees which map squares are requested and your IP address, but never receives the route itself or anything else about you.

Purchases. Subscriptions are handled by Google Play. Stride uses RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active; RevenueCat receives your Stride account ID and your purchase details from Google Play, not your health data. Stride never sees your payment details.

Services you connect. Health Connect, Google Health, Hevy and Google Calendar are off until you connect them. Stride reads your data from them on your phone. Google Calendar is the only one Stride writes to, when you or the coach add, move or delete an event.

Crash reports (Sentry). When Stride crashes or hits an unexpected error, it sends a report to Sentry, the crash-reporting service the developer uses: the error message and where in the code it happened, the app version and update, the phone model and Android version, and a random ID for this install that is not linked to your account. It never includes your logs, health or food data, chats, photos, email or name, and error messages are shortened and stripped of keys and sign-ins before they are sent. Turn it off in Settings → About & privacy → Send crash reports.

Stride has no analytics or ads, and never sells your data or shares it with anyone else.

Health Connect

With your permission, Stride reads exercise sessions and routes, distance, speed, steps and cadence, elevation and floors, calories burned, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, VO2 max, oxygen saturation, respiratory rate, sleep, weight and body fat from Health Connect. It uses this data only to show your training, recovery and body trends and to give the AI coach context for its advice, as described above. Stride does not write to Health Connect. Data read from Health Connect is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with anyone except as described above for the AI coach. Stride's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Not medical advice

Stride and its coach give general fitness and nutrition information. They are not a medical device and do not diagnose or treat anything. For pain, injury, illness, pregnancy, or concerns about eating, talk to a doctor or other qualified professional.

Age

Stride is for adults aged 18 and over.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: in the app, Settings → Account → Delete account. Without the app, see Delete your account. This removes your email, password, subscription status and usage count from Stride's server.
  • Delete everything on your phone: Settings → Backup & data → Delete all my data, or uninstall the app.
  • Export: Settings → Backup & data → Export saves your data to a file you control.
  • Disconnect any service from Settings → Integrations, and revoke Stride's access in that service's own settings.

Security

All data sent between the app and Stride's server, Google and other services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
