Delete your Stride account

Stride is made by heeeelyeeee. You can delete your Stride account and its data in the app or by email.

In the app

Open Stride → Settings → Account → Delete account, and confirm. This takes effect immediately.

Without the app

Send an email from the address you signed up with to ugleskippy123@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Stride account". We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your email address and password hash
  • Your subscription status
  • Your coach request count

Nothing is kept after deletion. Stride's server holds no other data about you.

What is not on our server

Your logs, chats, photos and health data are stored only on your phone. Remove them with Settings → Backup & data → Delete all my data, or by uninstalling the app.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
