Delete your Stride account

Stride is made by heeeelyeeee. You can delete your Stride account, and the data Stride's server holds about you, in the app or by email.

In the app

Open Stride → Settings → Account → Delete account, then confirm. This takes effect immediately.

Once the account is deleted, Stride asks you to sign in before it opens. If you also want your data removed from the phone, do that first: Settings → Backup & data → Delete all my data. That also withdraws your agreement, so Stride then shows the agreement screen and the set-up guide again. Agree again, which turns crash reports back on, and skip the guide to get back to Settings → Account.

Without the app

Send an email from the address you signed up with to ugleskippy123@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Stride account". We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

From Stride's server, together with the account:

  • Your email address and password hash
  • Your plan: the date you signed up, whether you have Stride Pro and when it ends
  • Your daily coach request, meal photo and goal setup counts
  • Your sign-in sessions, with the IP address and client label recorded for each

What is kept, and where

Deleting the account does not reach these. Each is yours to remove, or sits with the service named:

  • Your data on your phone: your logs, chats, photos, health data, GPS routes and settings stay until you remove them. In the app, use Settings → Backup & data → Delete all my data. Do this before you delete the account, because afterwards Stride asks you to sign in before it opens and you can no longer reach it. Delete all my data also withdraws your agreement, so you have to agree again (which turns crash reports back on) and can skip the set-up guide before you reach Settings → Account. If the account is already deleted, uninstall Stride, or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Stride.
  • Copies you made: backup files in a backup folder you chose, exports you saved or shared, and Android's Google backup of Stride, which a reinstall can restore. Delete these yourself, in the folder or in your phone's Google backup settings.
  • Google Play: your subscription (see below) and Google's own purchase records, under Google's policies.
  • RevenueCat: the customer record for your account ID, with your purchase history, under its own policy. Email us and we will have RevenueCat delete it.
  • Sentry: crash reports, kept under Sentry's retention. They carry a random install ID and are not linked to your account.
  • Anthropic (the AI coach chat): what the coach sent to Anthropic is governed by Anthropic's terms. Stride's server does not keep it, so Stride cannot delete it. Anthropic says it deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days, and may keep a request its automated safety systems flag for up to 2 years.
  • Google (voice memos, meal photos, app screenshots, form-check videos, meal stat estimates and updates to the coach's notebook): what Stride sent to Google's Gemini API is governed by Google's terms. Stride's server does not keep it, so Stride cannot delete it. Form-check videos are deleted by Google automatically after about 48 hours.
  • Technical logs: Supabase, Expo, Stadia Maps and Open Food Facts may keep logs of requests, such as your IP address, on their own schedules.
  • Connected services: anything inside Health Connect, Hevy or Google Calendar stays there. Remove Stride's access in each service's own settings.

The privacy policy has the full list of what Stride collects and where it goes.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription, and Google Play keeps charging you until you cancel. Cancel it in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
